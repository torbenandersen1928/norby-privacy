2. Who can join

  • You must be 18 or over.
  • One account per person, in your own name or a name your friends would recognise. Don't pretend to be someone else.
  • Keep your password to yourself. If you think someone else has got into your account, tell me straight away.

3. What's yours

Everything you make stays yours. Your loaves, recipes, methods, bakes and photos belong to you, and anything you don't share never leaves your phone (and your own iCloud).

When you share something (a bake, its photo and caption, and, if you choose, its recipe and method; your profile name and photo; starter names on a family tree), you give me permission to store it, copy it, resize it and show it to other signed-in bakers in Nørby, for as long as it's shared. This permission is free, isn't exclusive (you can share your bakes anywhere else too), and ends when you unshare it or delete your account. It doesn't let me sell your work or use it in advertising. If I ever want to show your bake outside the app, I'll ask you first.

You can only share what's yours to share: your own photos, and nobody else's recipe passed off as your own.

Other bakers can see what you share and may learn from it. That's the point. Please don't copy someone else's shared bakes outside Nørby without asking them.

One exception, for family trees. A starter that other bakers' starters came from stays on the tree after you delete your account, kept by "a baker", with its name, because other people's lineage depends on it. Your name goes; the starter's name stays.

If you see something like this, report it (••• on the bake, comment or profile) and block the person if you want. I read every report within 24 hours and will remove the content, and may suspend or remove the account, without warning when it's serious.

5. Gifting starters

Nørby records where a starter came from. It doesn't send anything. Swapping a jar of starter happens between you and another baker, in real life, and is your own arrangement. Take the usual care meeting someone you don't know well, and the usual kitchen hygiene with food you give or receive. I can't check anyone's starter, kitchen or intentions.

6. Using Nørby fairly

Don't try to break, overload or get round Nørby's limits; don't use other people's accounts; don't use automated tools to make accounts, copy content or read the service in bulk; don't use Nørby to collect information about other bakers.

7. Reports, complaints and how I keep Nørby safe

  • Reporting: ••• → Report on any bake, comment or profile. Four quick choices and an optional note. I read every report within 24 hours.
  • Blocking: ••• → Block. Neither of you sees the other's profile, bakes or follows any more, and they aren't told.
  • Complaints and appeals: if you disagree with something I've removed, an account I've suspended, or how I handled a report, email norbybakes@gmail.com and I'll reply within 14 days.
  • Illegal content: I remove illegal content as soon as I know about it, and I keep a written note of how I look after safety on Nørby, as the UK's Online Safety Act asks.
  • No automatic scanning: Nørby doesn't use any automated tool to scan photos or words. I review reports myself.
  • Who is responsible: I'm the person accountable for safety on Nørby. You can reach me at norbybakes@gmail.com.
